# 🏙️ CAPITAL KINGDOM
### *From Humble Beginnings to a Trillion-Dollar Urban Empire*

---

## 🌟 Tagline
> **Build, invest, survive, and rule! Buy rental apartments, run factories & oil fields, host stadium championships, manage the grand city park, and maintain your vitality in a living, breathing low-poly metropolis.**

---

## 📖 About the Game
**Capital Kingdom** is an immersive city tycoon, real estate empire builder, and urban life simulation developed for the Global Game Jam. Step into a vibrant, bustling 3D metropolis where every building has a purpose, every citizen has a routine, and every financial decision shapes your destiny.

Start with modest savings, invest strategically in small apartment flats, collect daily rents, and steadily acquire prime commercial, industrial, and entertainment landmarks. But wealth isn't your only concern—manage your personal vitality, eat at local cafes, and visit the hospital when illness strikes. 

Can you balance property maintenance, emergency health crises, and aggressive investment to become the ultimate Capital Tycoon?

---

## ✨ Core Gameplay Features

### 🏢 Real Estate & Multi-Tier Rental System
- **Large Apartment Towers (20–30 Flats)**: High-yield residential skyscrapers. Purchase individual apartments to earn steady daily rent, or buy out the entire building for massive capital dominance.
- **Small Apartment Buildings (8–10 Flats)**: Perfect entry-level residential investments for early-game income compounding.
- **Property Ownership & Upgrades**: Acquire full ownership of commercial and municipal properties across the city.
- **Dynamic Maintenance System**: Owned properties incur realistic periodic maintenance expenses—the bigger and more luxurious the building, the more upkeep required to keep it in peak operating condition!

### 🏟️ Sports, Energy & Industrial Empires
- **Metropolitan Stadium**: Host football and athletic championships. Receive random match booking requests and cash in on lucrative matchday ticket payouts.
- **Championship Tennis Court**: Book professional tennis tournaments and collect court hosting fees.
- **Oil Stations**: Invest in energy infrastructure, tap oil reserves, and collect heavy petroleum revenue.
- **Industrial Factories**: Own automated production plants that generate goods and trigger lucrative scheduled collection events.

### 🌳 The Grand City Park & Dedicated Visitor AI
- **Massive Recreation Landmark**: Buy the iconic central city park featuring historic cathedrals, lush gardens, scenic trees, and festive attractions.
- **Dedicated Visitor Waypoints**: Over 44 dedicated AI waypoints guide park visitors along scenic nature trails and walking circuits.
- **Park Entry Fees**: Earn recurring ticket income as crowds flock into your park.

### 🍽️ Dining & Vitality Survival
- **Cafes & Restaurants**: Visit eateries throughout town to grab meals and refreshing drinks, restoring your energy.
- **Health & Hunger Depletion**: Watch your health bar—skipping meals depletes your vitality over time.
- **Realistic Starvation & Illness**: Low health triggers sickness and starvation debuffs, accompanied by an immersive screen-darkening and tunnel-vision vignette effect.

### 🏥 Capital General Hospital & Condition-Based Healthcare
- **Emergency Medical Center**: Visit the hospital anytime you fall ill or sustain critical exhaustion.
- **5-Tier Dynamic Severity Pricing**:
  - *Mild Fatigue (75–99 HP)*: Quick checkup & vitamins ($55).
  - *Moderate Fatigue (45–74 HP)*: Restorative energy therapy ($130).
  - *Moderate Illness (28–44 HP / Sickness)*: Antibiotics and medical treatment ($280).
  - *Serious Illness (< 28 HP)*: Intensive emergency care for acute starvation and malnutrition ($480).
  - *Critical Shock (< 12 HP)*: Full ICU resuscitation and recovery ($750).
- **VIP Hospital Owner Privilege**: Buy the hospital itself to receive **100% complimentary free healthcare** anytime!

### 🚕 Dynamic Transportation, Traffic & Rail Networks
- **Taxi Service Fleet**: Purchase the Taxi Station to deploy commercial taxis across city streets.
- **Smart Vehicle AI**: Realistic vehicle navigation, collision prevention, glowing headlights, taillights, and acceleration audio.
- **3D Spatial Rail System**: Moving trains traverse elevated city rail tracks with spatial 3D train sound effects.

### 🌙 Dynamic Day/Night Cycle & Visual FX
- **Living City Atmosphere**: Smooth transitions between bright daytime and atmospheric nightscapes.
- **Dynamic Illumination**: Street lamps automatically illuminate roadways, vehicle headlights pierce the dark, and building windows glow at dusk.
- **Custom Selection Shader**: Clicking any building activates an elegant visual highlight overlay without replacing the building's underlying architecture.
- **3D World Indicators**: Intuitive visual markers float above actionable buildings and landmarks.

### 🔔 Live City Occurrence Notification Bar
- A slick, real-time alert ticker situated at the top-right of your screen.
- Alerts you instantly about maintenance bills, illness emergencies, tournament booking requests, rent availability, and production payouts.

### 💾 Seamless WebGL Browser Persistence
- Built with custom browser storage serialization—your money, owned properties, flat portfolios, day/night clock, and character health are automatically saved and restored when playing on WebGL!

## 💡 Tycoon Strategy Tips
1. **Never Skip Meals**: Keep an eye on your HP bar in the top HUD. If it dips below 25 HP, you will contract starvation illness and require emergency hospital treatment!
2. **Compound Early Rent**: Buy individual flats in small rental houses early on. The steady stream of rent provides stable cash flow before you attempt to buy massive commercial properties.
3. **Budget for Maintenance**: As your empire grows, ensure you maintain a cash cushion. Expensive factories and stadiums require routine maintenance payments.
4. **Diversify Revenue**: Don't rely solely on rent—combine stadium matches, park admissions, factory output, and oil collection for continuous income spikes.
5. **Hospital Ownership is Ultimate Insurance**: Buying the hospital is expensive, but free healthcare forever ensures you'll never worry about medical bills again.
